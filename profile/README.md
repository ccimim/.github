The **[Centre for Computational Imaging and Modelling in Medicine (CIMIM)](https://www.cimim.org/)** at [The University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/) advances precision medicine through computational innovation, collaboration and data-driven discovery, for the benefit of all.

### Research directions

- **Computational phenomics** — integrating biological, clinical and imaging data at scale to characterise human phenotypes, uncover disease mechanisms and enable precision medicine.
- ***In silico* science** — building and applying computational models and simulations to predict disease, evaluate interventions and accelerate healthcare innovation.

### About this organisation

This is where CIMIM researchers publish code, pipelines and data tools. Each repository states its own licence, maintainers and how to cite it.

CIMIM is a cross-faculty centre within the [Christabel Pankhurst Institute](https://www.pankhurst.manchester.ac.uk/), spanning the Faculty of Science and Engineering and the Faculty of Biology, Medicine and Health.
